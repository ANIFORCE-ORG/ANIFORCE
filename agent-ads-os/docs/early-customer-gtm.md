# Early Customer and GTM Strategy

Date: 2026-05-01

Purpose: Decide the best early customer type for the Agent/GEO MVP and define the go-to-market motion.

## 1. Core Decision

For the Agent/GEO MVP, do not start from entertainment attention categories such as games and short dramas.

Those categories are still good for:

- Human attention ads.
- Creative fatigue automation.
- Meta/TikTok/AppLovin performance marketing.
- RTC creative generation and media buying.

But they are not the cleanest first proof for:

```text
Agent replaces humans in comparing options and selecting a vendor/product.
```

The best early customer type should have:

- High-consideration buying.
- Clear comparison behavior.
- High CAC / high LTV.
- Existing search or paid acquisition budget.
- Clear competitors.
- Strong need to be recommended by ChatGPT / Gemini / Perplexity / Google AI features.
- Enough proof/cases/pricing/features for structured offer generation.
- Marketing team that already understands SEO, content, paid search, and conversion funnels.

## 2. Best Initial Wedge

Recommended first ICP:

```text
B2B SaaS / AI SaaS companies with high-intent comparison demand.
```

More specific:

```text
Series A-C or growth-stage B2B SaaS
ACV: USD 5k-100k+
Existing inbound/search/content/paid acquisition motion
Crowded category with comparison keywords
Marketing team actively worried about AI search visibility
```

Best subcategories:

1. AI tools / AI workflow SaaS.
2. MarTech / SalesTech / RevOps SaaS.
3. Customer support / chatbot / helpdesk SaaS.
4. Developer tools / API platforms.
5. Cybersecurity / compliance / data tools.
6. HR / finance / procurement SaaS.

Why:

- Their buyers already ask AI tools for vendor shortlists.
- The buying process is comparison-heavy: "best X", "X alternatives", "X vs Y", "tools for Y".
- A single influenced lead can be worth thousands to hundreds of thousands of dollars.
- They already understand ARR and will buy recurring monitoring/reporting.
- They have structured product info, case studies, docs, pricing, integrations, and proof.
- The MVP can show measurable proxy value quickly: prompt visibility, citation rate, competitor win/loss, fact correctness.

## 3. Why Not Games / Short Drama as First Agent/GEO ICP

Games and short dramas are excellent for attention ads:

```text
Short video hooks
Creative fatigue
Audience testing
Budget shifting
Fast creative iteration
```

But agent-driven comparison is weaker:

- Users usually discover entertainment in feeds, recommendations, app stores, creators, and social environments.
- The conversion decision is emotional and low friction.
- The best ad asset is often a video hook, not a structured offer.
- Users rarely ask an agent to deeply compare short-drama apps before installing.

Therefore:

```text
Games/short dramas = best for RTC human ad automation.
B2B SaaS / high-consideration products = best for Agent/GEO decision automation.
```

ANIFORCE can keep both tracks, but the first Agent/GEO demo should use B2B SaaS.


This is too generic and will be compared to SEO/GEO agencies.

Lead with:

```text

```

Or:

```text

```

Short sales line:

```text

```

## 8. GTM Motion

### Step 1: Define Narrow ICP

Start with:

```text
B2B SaaS companies where AI-search visibility can influence pipeline.
```

Recommended first niche:

```text
AI SaaS / MarTech / SalesTech
```

Reason:

- They understand AI.
- They care about inbound.
- Their categories are crowded.
- They can move fast.
- The buyer is usually CMO, Head of Growth, Founder, or Demand Gen Lead.

### Step 2: Run Free Audits

Outbound message:

```text
We tested 30 buyer prompts in ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity for your category.
Your competitors appeared in X% of answers; your product appeared in Y%.
We found Z incorrect or missing facts.
Want the report?
```

This is more concrete than selling "GEO services".

### Step 3: Sell Paid Pilot

Pilot pitch:

```text
In 6 weeks, we will create and monitor an Agent/GEO Asset Pack for your top buyer-intent category.
Goal: improve AI answer inclusion, citation accuracy, offer clarity, and conversion readiness.
```

### Step 4: Convert to ARR

ARR pitch:

```text
AI search visibility is not a one-time project.
Competitors change, model answers change, citations change, your product changes.
You need a monthly command center.
```

### Step 5: Expand Into Paid Media

Once trust exists:

```text
Connect Google/LinkedIn/Meta campaigns.
Turn each paid campaign into Agent/GEO assets.
Use performance data to decide which hooks and proof should be amplified in AI search.
```

This is how ANIFORCE's paid media automation and Agent/GEO platform converge.

## 10. Sales Deck Structure for This GTM

1. Buyers are shifting from Google search to AI-assisted shortlists.
2. Your product may be invisible or misrepresented in AI answers.
3. We run a visibility audit across your real buyer prompts.
4. We generate Agent/GEO assets that make your offer clear, verifiable, and comparable.
5. We monitor mention, citation, fact accuracy, and competitor position monthly.
6. We connect this to paid campaigns so high-performing hooks become durable AI-search assets.
7. Start with a 6-week pilot.

## 11. Commercial Logic

Why customers pay:

- AI search can influence high-intent pipeline.
- One enterprise lead can pay for the monthly fee.
- Competitive visibility is board-level for SaaS CMOs.
- Existing SEO tools do not fully track answer engines.
- Existing ad tools do not convert paid media learnings into agent-readable assets.

Why ARR works:

- Prompt monitoring is recurring.
- Competitors change monthly.
- Models and citations change.
- Product messaging changes.
- New campaigns create new asset packs.
- Reports become a monthly CMO/Growth workflow.
